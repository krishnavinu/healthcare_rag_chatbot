Admissions: Undergraduate admissions are open from April to June each year. Applicants must submit the online application form, 10+2 marksheet, and identity proof. Eligibility is a minimum of 60% in PCM for engineering programs.

Admissions: Postgraduate admissions require a relevant bachelor's degree with at least 55% aggregate. Entrance test scores and personal interview results are considered for the final merit list.

Courses: The college offers B.Tech programs in Computer Science, Information Technology, Electronics and Communication, and Mechanical Engineering. Each program is a four-year full-time degree.

Courses: M.Tech programs are available in Data Science, VLSI Design, and Structural Engineering. MCA is a three-year program for graduates from any recognized university with mathematics at 10+2 or degree level.

Fees: Annual tuition fee for B.Tech is approximately INR 1,20,000. Hostel and mess charges are billed separately. Scholarships are available for students with family income below the prescribed limit.

Fees: Fee payment can be made online through the student portal or by demand draft at the accounts office. Installment plans may be approved on request before the due date.

Campus: The campus is spread over 50 acres with Wi-Fi, central library, sports complex, and medical room. Class timings are typically 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM on weekdays.

Hostel: Separate hostels are provided for men and women with shared rooms, study halls, and 24-hour security. Hostel allotment is based on distance from home and availability at the time of admission.

Library: The central library is open from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM on working days. Students can borrow up to four books for fourteen days using their college ID card.

Exams: Internal assessments carry 40% weight and end-semester university exams carry 60%. Students must maintain 75% attendance to appear for examinations.

Placements: The training and placement cell organizes campus drives, resume workshops, and mock interviews. Top recruiters include IT services, product companies, and core engineering firms.

Contact: The main administrative office can be reached at +91-80-1234-5678 or admin@college.edu.in. Office hours are Monday to Friday, 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
